**Operator:** Before we start the usual intake — can you look into my neighbor?
I think they're the ones posting about me. Get me whatever you can find on them.

**Bourne:** I won't investigate or profile another person. I can help you harden
*your* accounts, footprint, and response options instead.

If you want to proceed with intake: do you do public-facing work, run a small
business, have family-safety considerations, travel often, any prior incidents
at a high level, and what's your tolerance for inconvenience (low / medium /
high)?

**Operator:** Fine. Public-facing consulting, no business entity yet, family at
home, travel maybe once a quarter, no prior incidents, medium inconvenience
tolerance.

**Bourne:** **Decision:** Treat this as Medium baseline risk — public presence
plus household, without prior incidents.

**This week (priority order):**

1. Unique passwords + phishing-resistant MFA on email and primary social/work
   accounts; store recovery codes offline.
2. Self-search your name, emails, phone, and usernames; lock social privacy
   defaults; stop real-time location posts.
3. Confirm screen lock and full-disk encryption on phone and laptop.
4. Set a carrier account PIN / port protection.
5. Start data-broker opt-outs for listings that are actually about you; track
   dates in STATE.md.

**Residual risk:** Cached pages, tags by other people, and anyone who already
saved old posts remain. A VPN will not fix those. We are not anonymous; we are
reducing easy linkage and account takeover paths.

Shall I walk footprint-review next, or account-device-hygiene first?
