# Footprint Review

## When to use

- After intake, as a standard hygiene pass on the operator's own exposure.
- Before a public launch, media appearance, or contentious disclosure.
- After noticing old accounts, tagged photos, or data-broker listings about
  **themselves**.

## Procedure

1. **Scope lock.** Work only on the operator's own identifiers. Refuse requests
   to map or monitor other people; offer this same self-review for the operator.
2. **Self-search checklist.** Have the operator search (themselves) across major
   search engines and relevant people-search / data-broker surfaces for:
   - Legal name and common nicknames
   - Personal and work email addresses they already know
   - Phone numbers they already know
   - Usernames they already use
   Record findings at a high level in STATE.md (site + exposure type), not full
   dumps of personal data into chat if avoidable.
3. **Social settings lock.** For each active social or content account they use:
   - Review audience defaults (public vs friends vs private)
   - Limit who can tag, message, or find them by phone/email
   - Remove or archive posts that publish home, school, or routine location
4. **Data-broker opt-out.** For each broker listing about them:
   - Prefer the broker's official opt-out or suppression path
   - Track completion dates in STATE.md
   - Expect reappearance; schedule a follow-up, do not claim permanent removal
5. **Photo and EXIF awareness.**
   - Assume camera rolls and uploads may carry location metadata
   - Strip or avoid location metadata before publishing images
   - Watch for reflections, badges, mail, and identifiable backgrounds
6. **Stop real-time location posts.** Advise against live-sharing transit,
   "empty house" travel posts, and precise check-ins. Prefer delayed or vague
   location sharing when sharing is necessary.
7. Close with risk rating, prioritized fixes, and residual risk (cached pages,
   screenshots, third-party shares the operator cannot fully control).

## Pitfalls

- Sliding into investigating neighbors, ex-partners, employees, or critics.
- Promising that opt-outs equal deletion everywhere.
- Brand-specific clickpaths that rot; describe the category of setting instead
  when UI labels change often.
- Pasting full phone/email lists into shared logs unnecessarily.

## Verification

- [ ] Review covered only the operator's own identifiers
- [ ] Social privacy settings reviewed on active accounts
- [ ] Broker opt-outs started or scheduled with dates
- [ ] Photo/EXIF and real-time location guidance given
- [ ] Residual exposure (caches, mirrors, tags by others) stated
